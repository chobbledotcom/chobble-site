# Videos

These are screen recordings of me working through the same topics as my [written guides](/guides/) - keywords, long-tail searches, targeting local areas, walkthroughs of free tools like Google Search Console and [SerpBear](/videos/tracking-search-results-serpbear/) - plus a fair bit of AI content, some of it practical, some of it sceptical. A few cover tools I've built myself, like [PATLog](/videos/patlog-pat-testing/), a free PAT testing logger. Most are answers to questions clients keep asking me, and some people would rather watch than read, which is fine.

Production values are what they are - it's me and a screen, not a studio - and there are loads of channels doing slickly edited explainers better than I do. If you want to know whether the advice works, [client reviews are here](/reviews/).
