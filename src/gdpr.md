# Make a GDPR request

If you want to know what personal data I hold about you, get a copy of it, have it corrected or deleted, or object to the way I'm using it, you can ask me using the form below. Chobble CIC is the data controller, and I handle requests myself.

Under UK GDPR I have to respond within one month of getting your request, though I might ask you to confirm your identity first if I can't tell who you are from the request itself. If the request is complicated I may extend that by up to two months, but I'll tell you if that happens and why.

The form sends your answers to me through [Formspark](https://formspark.io), with spam filtering from [Botpoison](https://botpoison.com), which is the same setup as the contact form - [the privacy page](/privacy/) explains what happens to that data. You don't have to use the form; you can email [hello@chobble.com](mailto:hello@chobble.com), or call or text 07441 125 952 instead. If you're not happy with how I've handled your data, you can [complain to me](/complaints/) first or go straight to the [ICO](https://ico.org.uk/).

The questions below change depending on the kind of request you pick, so some of them will only appear once you've chosen an option. Pick the closest one and add the detail in whichever boxes appear.
