# Web services for Prestwich companies

I'm Prestwich born and raised, and I build websites and offer tech help to local businesses, charities, and community groups. If you need someone local who can explain things without jargon and won't lock you into a platform you can't get out of, I'm probably a good fit.

I work with sole traders working from home, shops in the village that want more footfall, mobile tradespeople who visit customers at their addresses, and community groups that need an online presence whatever their budget. If you're not sure whether what you need is something I do, ask - if it's not, I probably know someone round here who can help.

Paid work round here has included the site [Renegade Solar](/examples/renegade-solar/), the solar panel installers, run on - they now sit near the top of Google for "solar panel installer Prestwich" - and alongside that I've been supporting charities and community groups round Prestwich for 14+ years, including [Blue Pits Housing Action](/examples/blue-pits/), and running [Vegan Prestwich](/examples/vegan-prestwich/), the local vegan directory my wife and I have kept going since 2018. ## The technical setup

Everything I build is static: you edit plain text files (or use a visual editor, or just send me the changes), the site rebuilds itself, and hosting through Bunny.net's CDN costs pennies a month. There's no database, no plugins, and nothing to patch, which is why the sites load quickly and keep loading quickly - the same boring Eleventy-based setup that all of [the examples](/examples/) I publish run on, with the code available on [my Git forge](https://git.chobble.com) if you want to read it.
