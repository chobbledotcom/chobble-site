## Websites for Rossendale businesses

Rossendale is a valley of small towns - Haslingden, Rawtenstall, Bacup, Whitworth, Crawshawbooth, Waterfoot, Helmshore - and a lot of the businesses here are small enough that the owner is also the person who answers the phone. A website doesn't need to be complicated to help with that. It needs to show what you do, where you are, when you're open, and how to get in touch, and it needs to do it quickly on a phone.

People search for the town they're in, such as "driving lessons in Haslingden" or "cafe in Bacup", so a site that names the towns it covers gives Google more to work with. Some businesses need a page for each town. Others need a smaller site, but I'd still start with at least three pages: home, reviews and contact.
