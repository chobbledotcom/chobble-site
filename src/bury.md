## What I build

I build static sites. The pages are generated in advance and served as plain files, so they load quickly and cost very little to host. There's no database running behind them and no plugins to update, which means fewer things to go wrong and less for you to pay someone to maintain.

You edit the site through [PagesCMS](https://pagescms.org/) in your browser, or by changing plain text files if you're comfortable with that. If you'd rather send me the changes, that's fine too. You own the full source code, so you're never stuck with me if you want to move to another developer or host it yourself.

Hosting costs **£10/month** without support, or **£40/month** with updates, monitoring and advice, and charities and the other discounted groups pay half. The sites run on [Bunny.net](/hosting/), a European hosting provider, so they stay quick wherever your customers are.

## Websites for Bury businesses

Bury has a lot of businesses that live on footfall and local reputation: market traders, independent shops, cafes, tradespeople, and services that people find by asking around or searching on their phone. The site doesn't need to be clever to help with that, but it does need to answer those questions quickly on a phone. It should show what you sell, where you are, when you're open, and how to get in touch.

For a market trader, I'd still want at least three pages: a home page that says what you sell and where you trade, a reviews page, and a contact page. That will do more for you than a bigger site you don't have time to maintain. For a tradesperson, the important thing is usually showing the areas you cover, the work you've done, and the reviews you've collected, because people want to know you're local and reliable before they ring you.

Bury also has a lot of classes, clubs and events. If you run something like that, the tickets route below is often a better fit than a custom build, because it gives you a public page and a booking system without paying for a full website first.
