## Full site SEO audits

A full site audit means reading the whole website properly:

- I scrape your **entire site** - every page in your sitemap and every page I can find beyond it
- AI reads through the parsed content - your homepage, your service and product pages, your blog archive - with me steering it towards what matters and checking anything that looks odd, so the report is based on what's actually on your pages
- I check the technical layer: **titles, meta descriptions, headings, images** and the details Google shows about your pages
- I research your **off-site footprint**: directory listings, social profiles, review platforms, and old versions of your pages via the Internet Archive, because Google often remembers pages you've forgotten
- I compare everything against my own [published guides](/guides/) and the patterns I see across every audit I do, so you get advice matched to your type of business

You get a **written report** that gives you: the one headline problem holding the site back, a priority order split into this week / this month / this quarter, an estimated time for each fix, explicit "what NOT to do" warnings (because the fixes can go wrong too), and the one page on your site that everyone else should sound like.

You also get an **audio version** of the report, generated like a podcast episode, so you can listen to it in the car or on the school run.

If you use AI tools, I can include a **prompt file** written for your site, so you can use ChatGPT or Claude to help implement the fixes without them flattening your voice. See my guides on [using AI effectively](/guides/using-ai-effectively/) and [what AI gets wrong](/guides/critiquing-ai-output/).

For bigger sites, the audit doesn't have to stop at a to-do list. I've built tooling that pulls a site's content out of its CMS, works through it page by page with AI doing the legwork and me doing the checking, and pushes the corrections back - refreshing thin pages, adding the internal links between related pages that hold a site together, and catching the stock phrases that make copy sound machine-written. That's how a few hundred pages gets sorted in weeks. Ask about it if your site has outgrown fixing pages by hand.

Full audits are priced at my [flat hourly rate](/prices/). I'll tell you up front how long I expect your site to take, and anything beyond that gets discussed before I do it - no surprise invoices.
