## What I build

I build static websites. The pages are generated in advance and served as plain files, so there's no database to slow things down, no plugins to update, and no admin login on the site itself. They're quick, cheap to run, and they keep working.

You can edit the words and pictures yourself through [PagesCMS](https://pagescms.org/), which is a browser-based editor that looks a bit like a simple form. If you'd rather not touch it, you can send changes to me and I'll make them. The full source code is yours, and you can take it to another developer or host it yourself whenever you want.

## What local businesses need online

A lot of the businesses round here run on repeat custom and word of mouth, and that's not something a website can replace. What a website can do is give a new customer somewhere to check your opening hours, see what you stock or serve, and find your phone number without scrolling through old Facebook posts.

Crumpsall is small enough that people often find you on the map before they find your website, so your Google Business Profile and your site need to agree with each other. If someone searches for a shop in Crumpsall, Google will often show the map listing first, so it's worth making sure the address, opening hours and phone number match in both places. I can help you sort that out as part of the build, or point you at my [free guide to Google Business Profiles](/guides/google-business/) if you'd rather do it yourself.

It can also join things up. If you sell wholesale, a clear list of what you carry and a phone number that works is often more useful than a checkout. If you run a takeaway or a small service business, a simple site with your menu, prices and delivery area does the job.

I'll ask how your customers get in touch before I decide what the site needs. There's no point building an online shop if everyone rings you, and there's no point hiding your phone number if that's how you take orders.
