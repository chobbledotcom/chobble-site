## Saying Whitefield by name

Whitefield is its own town, separate from Prestwich and Bury, and people search for it by name. If your website only talks about "Manchester" or "north Manchester", someone in Whitefield has to guess whether you cover them. A page that mentions Whitefield, the roads you're near, and the places you serve makes it obvious, and it's the sort of thing Google uses to decide whether your site is relevant to a local search.

The useful version is to write something specific: where you are, how to get there, what you do for people in Whitefield, and maybe the bits of the area your customers would recognise. The shops along Bury New Road, the Metrolink stops at Whitefield and Besses o' th' Barn, the side streets off the main road - those are the details that make a page feel like it was written by someone who has been there.
